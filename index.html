What we pour

Batch Brew

Our daily filter. Smooth, clean, never bitter. Refills are half price.

Piccolo Latte

Small but mighty. Double ristretto with silky milk. The regulars' favorite.

Matcha Latte

Ceremonial grade, whisked properly. Not the sugary powder stuff.

Cold Brew

Steeped 18 hours. Served black or with a splash of oat milk.

Banana Bread

Baked here every morning. Walnuts, dark chocolate chips, no raisins.

Sea Salt Cookie

Chewy center, crispy edge, flaky salt on top. One is never enough.

We also have oat milk, almond milk, and soy milk. Just ask.

We opened because we were tired.

Tired of coffee shops that rush you. Tired of places where the music is louder than conversation. Tired of paying too much for a cup that tastes burnt.

So we built Coffe. A small spot with wooden tables, warm lighting, and the smell of fresh grounds. Nothing fancy. Just good coffee and a place to sit with it.
